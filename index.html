SAFETY CENTRE

You stay in control.

Block someone

Blocking immediately prevents matching and hides your profiles from one another.

Report a concern

Reports go to NANDAL’s moderation queue. We review harassment, fake profiles, spam, underage users, and inappropriate content.

In an emergency

NANDAL is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services if you believe someone is in immediate danger.

Delete your account

This immediately removes your photos, clears your profile details, and hides you from Discover. Existing matches will see your name as “Deleted user.” To remove your login credentials entirely, email privacy@nandal.app.
