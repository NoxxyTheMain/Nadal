Community standards

Last updated: August 2026

NANDAL is a space for adults to meet with intention. Everyone who joins agrees to treat others with respect and to help keep the community safe.

Who can join

NANDAL is for people aged 18 and over. Misrepresenting your age or identity is not allowed and may result in immediate removal.

Be authentic

  • Use recent photos that clearly show you.
  • Do not impersonate another person or create fake profiles.
  • Be honest in your bio and conversations.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Accept a pass or a no with grace — consent matters at every step.
  • Do not share another member’s private messages or photos without permission.

Keep it appropriate

  • No nudity or sexually explicit content in profile photos.
  • No spam, scams, solicitations, or off-platform payment requests.
  • Do not use NANDAL to promote illegal activity.

Stay safe

  • Meet in public when you decide to meet in person, and tell someone you trust.
  • Use block and report tools if something feels wrong.
  • NANDAL is not an emergency service — contact local authorities if someone is in immediate danger.

Reporting and enforcement

Members can report concerns from Discover or through our moderation queue. We review reports for harassment, fake profiles, nudity, spam, underage users, and other violations. We may warn, restrict, or permanently remove accounts that break these standards.

Questions

Reach us at safety@nandal.app.
